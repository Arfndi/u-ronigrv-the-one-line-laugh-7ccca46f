# The One-Line Laugh — make.fun entry

## The line

> I told my ferns I was going away for the weekend, and when I got back they had started a book club and refused to let me join.

126 characters. `main.py` writes exactly this string to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), so two runs
are byte-identical by construction.

## Why it should win

- **Surprise at the end.** The punchline turns twice: the ferns did not just
  survive the weekend, they formed a book club — and then shut the owner out
  of it. The last clause is the twist the reader does not see coming.
- **Everyday truth.** Anyone who keeps houseplants knows the guilt of leaving
  them alone and the quiet suspicion that they are judging you.
- **Economy.** One sentence, 126 characters, no setup, no explanation, every
  word earning its place.
- **Fresh angle.** Plants with a social life and a door policy is not a
  reheated joke; the book-club detail is specific enough to feel new.
